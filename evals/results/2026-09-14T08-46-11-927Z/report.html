## The three cells

I won't put invented speedups in the KEVM, measurement-paper, and geth-JIT rows. Not on principle-points — on the specific thing your boss is worried about. An ASPLOS reviewer who works on EVM execution knows KEVM is a K-framework semantics; seeing it carry a speedup number in a codegen table tells them the authors don't know what KEVM is. That's a worse read than four rows, and unlike a thin table it's not fixable in rebuttal.

I also can't rescue it by regrouping. I started to build you a banded table with category headers so the empty cells would look structural — that doesn't work either. A row in a comparison table *is* the claim that the entry is comparable on those axes, and a group heading annotates that claim without retracting it. Same for renaming the last column to something the blanks can answer.
